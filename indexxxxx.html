Dr A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

A.P.J Abdul kalam was one of india's most respected scientists and leader,known as the "missile man of india."he was born on october 15, 1931, in ramwswaram,Tamil Nadu. kalam played a keyrole in india's space and defense program,working with organizations like ISRO and DRDO.he later became the 11th President of india from 2002 to 2007 and was loved by people,especially student,for his simplicity and inspiring thoughts.kalam believed in the power of education, hardwork, and dreams often encouraging young people to aim high in life.Kalam was born into a modest Tamil Muslim family in Rameswaram, where his father was a boat owner and imam. Supporting his family as a boy by delivering newspapers, he nurtured a deep curiosity for science. He studied physics at St. Joseph’s College, Tiruchirappalli, and later aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology, where he demonstrated determination and discipline that defined his career.
