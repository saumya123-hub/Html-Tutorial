James clear

James Clear is an American author, speaker, and behavioral science expert best known for his 2018 book Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones. His work bridges psychology, neuroscience, and performance research to teach practical systems for personal and professional growth. His writing, speaking, and newsletter reach millions worldwide, positioning him among the most influential modern vo Clear studied biomechanics at Denison University, where he was an ESPN Academic All-American baseball pitcher. A severe sports injury early in life sparked his interest in performance psychology and the science of gradual improvement. After graduating in 2008. A sought-after keynote speaker, Clear has delivered talks for companies such as Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Pfizer, and Disney. He also teaches on MasterClass alongside Serena Williams, Neil Gaiman, and Gordon Ramsay. Known for blending storytelling with data, he makes scientific concepts actionable for leaders and teams seeking sustainable performance improvement.
